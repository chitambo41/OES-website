Nursery & Daycare Services

Opportunity Daycare and Nursery Center provides caring nursery education to children from 2 years old, structured into four developmental classes.

🍼

Baby Class

Specifically tailored for toddlers aged 2 to 4 years. Focuses on sensory play, foundational language, motor skills, and gentle social adaptation.

Ages 2–4 yrs
🎈

KG 1 Class

Introductory phonics, numbers, colors, shapes, and group activities designed to spark active curiosity and peer friendship.

Ages 4+ yrs
🎨

KG 2 Class

Advancing early reading, handwriting, creative arts, and foundational mathematical concepts through collaborative guided sessions.

Ages 4–5 yrs
🌟

KG 3 Class

Advanced kindergarten preparation that ensures a smooth, self-assured graduation into primary school with strong academic and social fluency.

Tuition Services for Higher Performance

Our qualified teachers cooperate closely with parents and students to significantly improve academic performance and national examination confidence.

📘

Primary Education Tuition

Dedicated tuition services for children from Standard 1 to Standard 7. Our professional teachers provide clear explanations, practice exercises, and exam techniques.

Subjects Offered:

  • Mathematics
  • English
  • Science
  • Geography
  • History
  • Kiswahili
🔬

Secondary Education Tuition

Supporting secondary school students at the critical level where they plan their future dreams. High quality, focused tuition at affordable costs.

Subjects Offered:

  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • Mathematics
  • Geography
  • History
  • English
🚀

Pre-Form One Program

A deep, structured introduction to secondary school subjects, held from September to December right after Standard 7 national examinations.

Subjects Covered:

  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • English
  • Geography
  • History
  • Kiswahili
🎯

Pre-Form Five Program

Advanced level preparatory coaching starting right after Form Four NECTA examinations in November to give students a head start for High School.

Advanced Subjects:

  • Mathematics

  • Biology
  • Geography
  • Economics
