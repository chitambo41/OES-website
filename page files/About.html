🏛️ School History & Identity

Our Story of Purpose & Passion

Established in 2025 by Opportunity Education Support (OES), Opportunity Daycare & Nursery Center was founded to support early childhood development by seamlessly combining quality education, creativity, and play-based learning.

How It Began

A Commitment to Every Child

Since its establishment, Opportunity Daycare and Nursery Center has remained steadfastly committed to helping children develop strong foundations in language, numeracy, social skills, and personal confidence.

Through the tireless dedication of our professional teachers and wonderful support from parents, the school continues to grow, providing a warm, welcoming environment where every child is seen, supported, and valued.
